The verifier also cannot check an ACT/360 result today. `verification/verify.py` sets `EXPECTED_DAY_COUNT = "ACT/365F"` and fails any other disclosed day count as blocking. That behaviour is correct.

**Not priced.** I did not substitute an ACT/365F price either, because that is not what was asked.

**Questions I would have asked:**
